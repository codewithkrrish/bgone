Add up to 10 photos above. AI mode handles busy backgrounds; Colour mode is instant on plain ones.

AI mode downloads a segmentation model (about 40 MB) on first use and caches it in your browser — later runs are fast. AI quality varies by photo: fine hair, glass and busy backgrounds are the hardest cases, and no tool gets them perfect every time. Colour mode only removes background that is connected to the photo's edges, so a matching colour inside the subject is kept. If the AI library cannot load (offline or blocked CDN), Colour mode still works fully.

How it works

Private by design

BGone decodes and segments everything with your browser's own APIs. There is no server upload, so there is nothing to leak, store, or queue behind.

Two ways to cut out

AI segmentation for real-world photos, and a flood-fill colour key for studio-style plain backdrops — with feathered edges so cuts don't look clipped.

Honest before & after

Click Compare on any result to drag a slider between the original and the cutout, and download it transparent or over a background of your choice.
